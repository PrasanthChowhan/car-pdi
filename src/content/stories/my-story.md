The dealer tried to scam us in more than one way.

The reason I am making this tool is to prevent what happened to me from happening to you.

I was looking to buy a car, and I specifically wanted it on July 9th for my sister's birthday. We looked at many cars and finalized two options, paying the booking amount for both: a Tata Punch EV and a VinFast VF6 Wind from separate dealers. Since I wanted the car on July 9th at any cost, I decided to go with whichever dealer could deliver it by that date.

The VinFast dealer gave us the commitment that they could deliver the car on July 9th. At first, they refused to allow a pre-delivery inspection (PDI). Later, they agreed, but on the condition that we couldn't bring an outside inspector. We agreed to that as well. 

However, when I went to inspect the car on July 8th, all my excitement vanished. The car looked old and had already been driven for 167 km. The interior was in poor condition, and the key fob was stained. Furthermore, the tires were already 10 months old, and the car itself was manufactured 6 months prior. In simple terms, they were trying to stick us with an old car.

Don't blindly trust sales guys. When confronted, they claimed they had no idea the car would be in that condition. When I asked about the 167 km usage, they brushed it off as \"normal.\" I flat-out refused the car.

After I refused, another sales representative approached me, saying, \"Sorry sir, we weren't aware that this specific car had been brought in. Please wait for two hours, and I will bring a new car from the stockyard.\"

When the new car arrived, it actually looked new:
1. The odometer reading was only 21 km.
2. The protective covers were still intact.
3. The tires were new, just 5 months old.

If you are not careful and the registration is completed, you cannot do anything; you just have to accept the car they give you. That's why I am making this tool—to ensure you don't get scammed.

And you might think the problem was solved there... but no. I ended up paying an extra 7,000 rupees simply because I wasn't aware of the rules. It is illegal for a car dealer to charge PDI fees.

So I made this tool to not let the things that happened to me happen to you.
